Privacy-first by design

Lucy keeps privacy simple.

Lucy has two parts: a local Mac desktop app and an optional Chrome extension called Lucy Bridge. Each has its own privacy policy so you can see exactly what each component does with your data.

Lucy Desktop Privacy Policy

Covers the Mac app: local chats, memories, settings, local AI models, optional tools, and how Lucy is designed to keep your workspace on your Mac.

  • Local-first app data
  • Memories and chat history
  • Optional model and internet-tool behavior
Read desktop policy
Lucy Bridge Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Covers the browser extension only: reading the active tab after you click the extension icon and sending the page URL, title, visible text, and supported structured page details to Lucy’s local bridge on your Mac.

  • User-clicked active tab access
  • Localhost bridge communication
  • No sale, sharing, or third-party transfer
Read extension policy

Simple summary

Lucy Desktop is designed as a local-first AI assistant. Your chats, memories, settings, and local model files are intended to live on your Mac unless you deliberately enable or use a feature that contacts another service.

Lucy Bridge does not read every tab or browse in the background. It reads the active tab only after you click the extension icon, then sends that content to http://127.0.0.1:8765 for the local Lucy app.
